- `2k_uranus.jpg`
- `2k_neptune.jpg`

The launcher crops these maps into circular planet illustrations and adds shading.
The orbital illustration uses compressed distances and display sizes. Initial
phases are composed for legibility and do not represent current sky positions.
Approximate orbital-period ratios use NASA Space Place:
https://spaceplace.nasa.gov/years-on-other-planets/en/

Solar System Scope describes its maps as based on NASA imagery, with artistic
adjustments and fictional fill where surface data is incomplete. These are
visualisation textures, not unmodified spacecraft photographs.
